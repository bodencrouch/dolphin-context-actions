Unpack or compress from the right-click menu.

Extract Here, extract to a folder, add files to 7z or zip, or hash a payload — without opening a separate archive window.

Cheap check: right-click a .zip → Archive → Extract Here. The files land next to the archive.

Needs 7z or Ark on PATH. Dynamic names like Add to "photos.7z" are in the optional KIO plugin from a source build; this listing is the service-menu install.
